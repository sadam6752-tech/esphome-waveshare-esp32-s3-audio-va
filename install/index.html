Waveshare Voice

Voice satellite firmware for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-AUDIO-Board, driven by ioBroker.assistant. Flash it from this page with Chrome or Edge on a computer; no ESPHome build needed.

With display

With the Waveshare 2.8inch Capacitive Touch LCD on the LCD connector.

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https.

Without display

The board alone: speaker, microphones, LED ring, buttons.

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https.

Tile panel

The Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2 (2 inch) as a wall or desk panel for ioBroker.satellite-display with the clock, four pages of tiles and the screen lock; with a microphone soldered on it also listens.

ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2

Optional: a MAX98357A amplifier on the header P2 plays streams from ioBroker.

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https.

With microphone

The same panel with an INMP441 microphone on the header (SCK GPIO15, WS GPIO9, SD GPIO11, L/R to GND, 3.3 V): also a voice satellite for ioBroker.assistant with the wake words ioBroker, Hey Bro and Alisa. The answer is spoken on the MAX98357A.

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https.

Steps

  1. Connect the board with a USB-C cable and press Install. If no port shows up, hold BOOT while plugging the cable in.
  2. After flashing, the page offers to set up Wi-Fi. With the display, Wi-Fi can also be set on the device: Settings (gear) > Wi-Fi. Without a network the board opens the access point Waveshare-VA Setup (the tile panel: Tile Panel Setup); join it with a phone and pick your network.
  3. In ioBroker.assistant, add the satellite (Satellites tab). Later updates show up there as the Firmware update. Add the tile panel in ioBroker.satellite-display (Devices tab, IP address, port 6053).
